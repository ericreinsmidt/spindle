Screenshots for the project README.

Taken on the device rather than in the Simulator, because the Simulator does not
show what the reflective screen actually looks like and the app runs inverted.

To take one: hold Lock and press Menu. The image lands in /Screenshots on the
data disk as a 400 by 240 GIF. Convert it to PNG and drop it in here under the
name the README expects.

  library.png       the album list, with a playlist row visible at the top
  nowplaying.png    a track playing, ideally one with artwork
  haring.png        the Haring visualizer
  koi.png           the Koi visualizer
