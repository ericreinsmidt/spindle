Checking a reported skip in Sara Bareilles / Love Song
2026-08-07

Skips were heard on the device, in the same places whether the track was played
from the album or from a playlist. Since both play the identical .pda, that put
the audio file itself under suspicion. It is not the audio. Recording this so
the same ground is not covered again.

What was established, in order:

  The source MP3 is intact. 320 kbps, 258.83 seconds, and ffmpeg decodes the
  whole thing without emitting a single error.

  pdc only repackages, it does not re-encode. The shipped .pda is 11,481,552
  bytes against 11,493,604 for the ADPCM WAV that ffmpeg produced, and the
  12,052 byte difference is exactly the WAV header plus its LIST/INFO chunk. The
  .pda begins "Playdate AUD" and the ADPCM payload follows. So reproducing
  ingest's ffmpeg step and decoding the result back is a faithful test of what
  is actually on the device.

  Round tripping source -> ADPCM -> PCM and comparing against the source
  decoded directly:

      median relative error in loud windows      0.0195
      zero runs of 32+ samples in the round trip 97
      ...of those sitting where the source was
         not already quiet                       0
      20 ms blocks near identical to the one
         before, which is what a stutter is      0
      block to block correlation, median         0.03

  The 146 brief level collapses are musical. They appear at the same timestamps
  and the same levels in the source, which is what a sparse piano ballad with
  gaps between vocal phrases looks like.

  Clusters of very large sample to sample steps, above 1.0 in a normalized
  signal, exist at 89.6s, 123.9s and 163.95s. They are present in the source as
  well, and this is a heavily limited master, so they are content.

Conclusion: the converted audio is a faithful reproduction of the source and the
source has no dropouts, stutters or splices. Whatever was heard was in playback.

The device was instrumented to record every frame gap over 40 ms alongside the
cost of each session write, on the theory that the thirty second session
heartbeat was stalling the fileplayer's reads from flash. The gaps turned out to
be somewhere else entirely and the problem then stopped reproducing, so it was
left there. If it returns, that instrument is the thing to rebuild, and the
session heartbeat is still the first thing worth ruling in or out.
